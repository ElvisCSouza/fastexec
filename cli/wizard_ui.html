Language servers (optional, third-party)

FastExec drives compilers through their own language servers (jump to definition, find references, real rename). They are separate programs with their own licences, pinned to an exact version. Nothing is downloaded unless you tick it here, and each line shows the version, the size, the origin and the licence before you decide. The ones already detected in your files come ticked; the rest stay unticked.
